Methodology: - **Design / identification**: Natural experiment using the University of Cape Town policy of random allocation of first-year students to residences and (in 8 residences with double rooms where wardens stated they randomized) to same-gender roommates. - **Data and sample**: One cohort of 2012 freshmen; baseline survey February 2012 (first week of the year) and follow-up September 2012 (before final exams). UCT admission score (APS, out of 600) rescaled by dividing by 1000. Household wealth = PCA index of durable-goods ownership per capita; consumption = last-month spending.
