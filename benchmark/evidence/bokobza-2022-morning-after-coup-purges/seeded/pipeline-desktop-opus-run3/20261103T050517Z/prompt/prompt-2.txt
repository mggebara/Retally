## Paper
Title: General Rights
Methodology: - **Data and sample:** The cabinet data come from WhoGov (Nyrup and Bramwell 2020), which is built from the CIA *Chiefs of State and Cabinet Members* directory and coded each July. Failed coups come from Powell and Thyne (2011), with Bjørnskov and Rode (2020) used for robustness. The sample covers 115 autocracies, 1967–2016. It is restricted to country-years with a **lagged (t−1) Boix–Miller–Rosato democracy score of 0**. Appendices C/M use alternative autocracy definitions: lagged DD and lagged Polity IV < 6. Some models lose observations because control coverage is incomplete.
- **Country-level estimating equation (H1):** TSCS OLS of P_it = φC_it + βX_it + α_i + λ_t + ε_it.
  - **Dependent variable:** the year-to-year cabinet replacement rate P_it, in [0,1], equal to 1 − retention_rate adjusted for cabinet size (`retention_rateadj_minister`). It is a raw share, not standardised; coefficients are percentage-point changes.
  - **Key regressor:** C_it, a failed-coup dummy (95 failed coups in the analyses). Because cabinets are coded each July, coups before 1 July count in year t and coups on or after 1 July count in t+1.
  - **Fixed effects:** country FE plus a full set of year dummies. Every specification is also re-run with **leader FE instead of country FE**.
  - **Standard errors and N:** SEs are clustered by country. There are up to 3,715 country-years.
- **Control sets (all lagged one year):**
  - "Base": log GDP per capita and log population (PWT 9.0).
  - "Main" (preferred): Base plus dummies for military dictatorship, party dictatorship and monarchy (Anckar & Fredriksson 2019).
  - Add-on models on top of Main:
    - Electoral: NELDA national elections and years since the last election.
    - Economic: GDP per capita growth and Ross oil income.
    - Instability: UCDP civil war onset, NAVCO nonviolent campaigns, COW MID onset and interstate war onset, and CNTS strikes.
    - "Purges MAs": 1-, 2- and 3-year moving averages of past replacement rates, effectively a lagged-DV model.
    - "All controls": every control above together.
- **Placebo tests and robustness:**
  - Placebos replace C_it with onsets of civil war, nonviolent campaigns, MIDs, interstate war and strikes, under both country and leader FE.
  - Robustness checks:
    - a conventional lagged coup variable;
    - effects by coup month;
    - regime-type interactions;
    - a personalism control;
    - dropping full-turnover cabinets (rate = 1);
    - dropping each leader's first year;
    - coup types (military, civilian, royal) from Bjørnskov–Rode.
- **Headline results:** The median replacement rate is 17.4% in non-coup years and 38.1% after a failed coup. The Base model with country FE gives about **+0.10**. Across all specifications the effect is **+0.08 to +0.11** and significant at 95%; full tables are in Appendix A.
- **Individual-level linear probability model (H2–H4):** F_ijt = φC + ζM + γ(C×M) + βX + α_i + λ_t + ε.
  - **Dependent variable:** F = 1 if the minister is purged, meaning absent from the dataset the next year. Reshuffles within the cabinet are not counted as purges.
  - **Controls and FE:** the Base controls (log GDP per capita and log population, lagged), cubic polynomials in the minister's years in office (t, t², t³; dropped when experience is the moderator), country FE, year FE, and country-clustered SEs.
  - **Sample:** 23,655 ministers and 95,751 minister-years, with up to 90,020 in the figures (88,094 non-coup and 1,926 coup minister-years).
- **Moderators M, entered one at a time:**
  - **Loyalty:**
    - experience, measured as years in cabinet;
    - party alignment: leader's party, another party, or unaffiliated.
  - **Responsibility:**
    - strategic portfolio type, such as defence or foreign affairs;
    - five ordered importance tiers (Appendix Q), excluding the leader.
  - **H4:** a four-group classification crossing loyalty with responsibility.
    - *Strong loyalty* means the same party as the leader and experience at or above the median under that leader-year.
    - *High responsibility* means a top minister, deputy PM, VP, PM, or a president who is not the leader.
  - **Exclusion:** junior and low-ranking posts are excluded from the experience-based tests.
  - **Reporting:** results are marginal effects of a failed coup by minister type, with 95% confidence intervals (Figures 3 and 4, Appendix K).

lag_var <- function(df, var, id, time) {
  df <- df[order(df[[id]], df[[time]]), ]
  key <- paste(df[[id]], df[[time]])
  prev <- paste(df[[id]], df[[time]] - 1)
  df[[paste0("lag__", var)]] <- df[[var]][match(prev, key)]
  df
}
