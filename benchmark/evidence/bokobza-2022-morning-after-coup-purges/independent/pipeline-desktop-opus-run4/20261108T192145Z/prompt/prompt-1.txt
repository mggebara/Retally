Methodology Summary: - **Data and sample:** WhoGov cabinet dataset (Nyrup & Bramwell 2020, built from the CIA *Chiefs of State and Cabinet Members* directory and coded for July of each year), merged with Powell & Thyne (2011) coup data. The sample covers 115 autocracies, 1967–2016. Autocracy means a country-year with a **lagged (t−1) Boix–Miller–Rosato democracy score of 0**. Country-level models use up to 3,715 country-years with 95 failed coups. Individual-level models use 23,655 ministers and 95,751 minister-years (Figure 3 notes up to 90,020). Variables that don't cover the full sample drop some observations.
- **Country-level estimating equation (H1):** a two-way fixed-effects OLS on time-series cross-section data: P_it = φ·C_it + β·X_it + α_i + λ_t + ε_it.
  - P_it is the year-to-year **cabinet replacement rate**, ranging 0–1 (1 − retention_rateadj_minister, adjusted for cabinet size).
  - C_it is a **failed-coup dummy**. Coups before 1 July count in year t; coups on or after 1 July count in year t+1.
  - The coefficient is in proportion units, so it reads as a percentage-point change. It is **not** standardised.
- **Fixed effects, controls and standard errors:** country fixed effects plus a full set of year dummies. Leader fixed effects replace country fixed effects in a parallel set of models. Standard errors are **clustered by country**. All controls are lagged one year.
  - **Base:** log GDP per capita and log population (Penn World Table 9.0).
  - **Main (preferred):** Base plus military, party and monarchy dictatorship dummies (Anckar & Fredriksson 2019).
  - Further specifications each add one set to Main:
    - **Electoral controls:** NELDA national elections and years since the last election.
    - **Economic controls:** GDP per capita growth and Ross oil income.
    - **Instability controls:** UCDP/PRIO civil war onset, NAVCO nonviolent campaigns, COW military dispute and interstate war onsets, and CNTS strikes.
    - **Purges MAs:** 1-, 2- and 3-year moving averages of past replacement rates.
  - An **All controls** model includes every set above.
- **Placebo and robustness tests:**
  - Placebos swap C_it for civil war onset, nonviolent campaigns, military disputes, interstate war and strikes.
  - Alternative coup data from Bjørnskov & Rode (2020), including splits into military, civilian and royal coups.
  - Alternative autocracy definitions: DD/Cheibub et al. and Polity IV below 6.
  - A conventionally lagged coup variable.
  - Dropping cabinets with a replacement rate of 1, and dropping each leader's first year in office.
- **Individual-level estimating equation (H2–H4):** a linear probability model: F_ijt = φC + ζM + γ(C×M) + βX + α_i + λ_t + ε.
  - F is 1 if the minister is purged, meaning absent from the cabinet the next year. Reshuffles within the cabinet do not count as purges.
  - The model uses the Base controls, country and year fixed effects, and a cubic in the minister's years in office (t, t², t³) for time dependence. The cubic is dropped when experience is the moderator.
  - Each characteristic M is interacted with the failed-coup dummy, one at a time.
  - The reported quantity is the marginal effect of a failed coup for each minister type (Figures 3–4; tables in Appendix K).
- **Minister characteristics:**
  - **Experience:** years in cabinet. Junior and low-ranking posts are excluded from these tests.
  - **Party affiliation:** leader's party, another party, or no party.
  - **Strategic portfolio:** for example defense or foreign affairs.
  - **Importance:** five ordered tiers, excluding the leader.
  - **Loyalty × responsibility groups (H4):** four groups.
    - Loyal means the same party as the leader and experience at or above the median for that leader's ministers in that year.
    - High responsibility means top minister, deputy PM, VP, PM or President, when that person is not the leader.
- **Evaluation:** coefficients are reported with 95% confidence intervals and judged by statistical significance. The headline finding is an 8–11 point rise in the replacement rate (Base with country fixed effects: about 0.10). Descriptively, the median replacement rate is 17.4% in non-coup years and 38.1% in failed-coup years. There is no train/test split or cross-validation, since this is causal regression inference.
