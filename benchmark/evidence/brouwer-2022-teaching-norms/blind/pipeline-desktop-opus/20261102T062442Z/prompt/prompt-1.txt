Methodology Summary: - **Design:** Pre-registered (AsPredicted #24270) natural field experiment with a 3×2 between-subject design. It was run on 30 days in May–June 2019 near 30 randomly selected public elementary schools in Lyon, with each school visited on at most one day. The sample is 601 parents: 301 Alone and 300 Child. One dimension is whether the parent is naturally accompanied by a child ("Child") or alone ("Alone"); targets were parents arriving at or leaving school in the morning (~7:45–9:00) and afternoon (~16:15–18:00). The other dimension is the scene, played by one of two trained actors (male/female, randomly alternated across schools): "Violation" (actor litters a banana peel in a plastic bag), "Help" (actor accidentally drops the contents of a bag) and "Violation + Help" (VH; litters, then drops the bag). Scenes were played in mixed order.
- **Outcomes (binary):** Punishment = 1 if the parent verbally and explicitly confronts the actor about the littering, 0 otherwise. Helping = 1 if the parent picks up at least one item or asks the child to, 0 otherwise; a child helping without the parent's prompting does not count. Indirect punishment is withholding help, measured as lower helping in VH than in Help.
- **Sample restrictions:** Targets were single adults walking alone or with one or more children. Not targeted: parents visibly in a rush, on the phone, pushing a stroller, on a bike, walking a dog, carrying something with both hands, or accompanying disabled children. Dropped: targets who said in the follow-up survey that they had no child at the school, and targets who did not observe the violation. The Punishment analysis uses only the Violation and VH scenes (~400 obs). The Helping analysis uses only the Help and VH scenes (~401 obs). One observation is dropped from the controlled specifications because the target's gender is missing.
- **Estimation:** Pooled OLS linear probability models, Table 2. Punishment (cols 1–2) or Helping (cols 3–4) is regressed on Child, VH and Child × VH. Child × VH is the key test of Conjecture 2 (indirect punishment); Child is the key test of Conjectures 1 and 3. Coefficients are in raw probability units (not standardised). There are no fixed effects.
- **Controls (preferred specs, cols 2 and 4):** Male Target (father), Male Actor, Morning, Witness (RA1 judged that another adult was observing and able to intervene), Rich IRIS/Rich area (the school's IRIS-area median income is above the city median), Rain and Hot (dummies). These are all Table 1 variables except Survey Response.
- **Inference:** Standard errors are clustered at the school level (30 clusters). The conditions are also compared non-parametrically with χ² tests: Child vs Alone within each scene, and Violation vs VH within each condition. Balance is checked with two-sided t-tests of equal means (Table 1).
- **Robustness and extensions:** Logit models instead of LPM (App. A.4.4). Alternative sample restrictions and definitions (App. A.4.5, Table A9), e.g. treating child-helping prompted by the parent as not helping. Child × Witness interactions, and Witness treated as a treatment within Alone (App. A.4.2). Exploratory heterogeneity by number, age and gender of children, actor and parent gender, and IRIS income (App. A.4.6). Timing effects and time slot × Child (App. A.4.7). Separate online vignette studies (Krupka–Weber norm elicitation, not pre-registered) and a video-based norm study (App. A.5–A.6) to rule out differences in perceived norms.
- **Not applicable:** There is no train/test split, cross-validation or hyperparameter tuning. Evaluation is by hypothesis tests on the treatment coefficients and on χ² comparisons of rates.
