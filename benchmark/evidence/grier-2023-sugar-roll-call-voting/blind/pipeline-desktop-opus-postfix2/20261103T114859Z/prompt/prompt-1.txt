Methodology Summary: - **Design / estimator:** Fixed-effects (conditional) logit on a two-period panel of U.S. House roll-call votes on two near-identical anti-sugar-subsidy amendments: the 2013 Pitts amendment (H.Amdt. 227 to H.R. 1947, vote of 20 June 2013) and the 2018 Foxx amendment (H.Amdt. 605 to H.R. 2, vote of 17 May 2018), treated as repeated votes on the same issue. Preferred model (Table 1, col. 1): **district fixed effects**. Robustness model (Table 1, col. 2): **incumbent (member) fixed effects**. Estimation was done in Stata. No train/test split or cross-validation.
- **Dependent variable:** binary, 1 = "aye" on the reform amendment (a vote against sugar interests), 0 otherwise. Vote data come from GovTrack.us.
- **Key regressor:** inflation-adjusted (real) sugar-industry campaign contributions to the House incumbent, in dollars, entered linearly (unstandardised). Built from Center for Responsive Politics individual-level data by summing contributions from all sugar-industry PACs plus individual contributions over $200 from people (or their family members) who work in the sugar industry. Baseline window: from one year before each vote through the end of that vote's calendar year (roughly 6/20/2012–12/31/2013 and 5/17/2017–12/31/2018).
- **Controls (preferred spec):** ACU ideology rating (1–100, higher = more conservative); tenure (seniority, from the ACU data); district poverty rate; % of the population with a bachelor's degree; median income; % of the population over 65 (Census Bureau); and a dummy for membership on the House Agriculture Committee, plus a constant. All enter in levels.
- **Sample:** All House districts and incumbents across the two votes (113th and 115th Congresses). Under fixed-effects logit, only units whose vote changed between 2013 and 2018 contribute. District FE: 93 vote-switching districts, 186 observations. Incumbent FE: restricted to incumbents who voted both times and switched their vote (53 members), with 106 observations reported.
- **Inference:** p-values come from robust (heteroskedasticity-robust) standard errors. Significance stars are at 10%, 5% and 1%. The paper also reports pseudo-R².
- **Post-estimation:** Marginal effects on the probability of voting for reform (sugar money, ideology, tenure) are plotted with 95% CIs using Stata's Marhis package. They are evaluated at 50 equally spaced values of the focal variable, with the other covariates kept at their observed values.
- **Robustness:**
  - (Table 3 / Appendix A.2) Alternative contribution windows: (a) calendar years 1/1–12/31/2013 and 2018; (b) the 12 months before each vote up to the vote date (6/20/12–6/20/13 and 5/17/17–5/17/18). Each is estimated with both the district-FE and the incumbent-FE models.
  - DW-Nominate in place of ACU.
  - A Republican dummy.
  - Other controls (unemployment, % rural).
  - Contributions aggregated by CRP election cycle (Appendix A.3).
