Methodology Summary: ## Methodology Summary

- **Estimation method**: Fixed-effects logit model with two specifications: (1) district fixed effects and (2) incumbent (member) fixed effects. Identification comes only from units whose vote changed across the two amendments (93 vote-switching districts; 53 vote-switching incumbents).

- **Estimating equation**: Dependent variable = 1 if the member voted "aye" on the sugar-reform amendment (against sugar interests), 0 otherwise. Key regressor = inflation-adjusted total sugar-industry contributions to the incumbent (sugar PAC contributions + sugar-industry individual contributions >$200), measured in dollar units (coefficients are unstandardized log-odds, e.g., –0.0006 per dollar in Table 1).

- **Controls / preferred specification**: ACU ideology score (robustness: DW-Nominate), tenure (years in office), poverty rate, % with bachelor's degree, median income, % population >65, and a dummy for House Agriculture Committee membership. Demographics come from U.S. Census; votes from GovTrack; contributions from Center for Responsive Politics.

- **Sample / observations**: Pooled data on the 2013 (Pitts) and 2018 (Foxx) sugar amendment votes. Estimation samples: 186 observations (district FE) and 106 observations (incumbent FE), restricted to units with changed votes by the FE logit. Antisugar contributions excluded because negligible.

- **Standard errors**: Robust standard errors (no clustering specified).

- **Preprocessing**: Contributions aggregated by incumbent and inflation-adjusted; primary time window = one year before vote through end of calendar year of vote.

- **Robustness checks**: (1) Alternative contribution windows — calendar year of vote only (2013; 2018), and 12 months prior to vote date (6/20/12–6/20/13; 5/17/17–5/17/18); (2) Incumbent FE in addition to district FE; (3) Alternative ideology measure (DW-Nominate); (4) Alternative control sets (unemployment, % rural).

- **Evaluation / reporting**: Pseudo-R² reported (0.745 district FE; 0.721 incumbent FE); marginal effects at 50 equally spaced values plotted via the Marhis Stata package with 95% CIs (Figures 1–3). No train/test split or cross-validation (observational causal-inference design).
