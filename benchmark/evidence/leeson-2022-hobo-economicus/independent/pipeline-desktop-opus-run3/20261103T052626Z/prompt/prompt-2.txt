Methodology: - **Data / samples.** (i) Station-visit panel: 242 visits to 26 "stations" (25 Washington DC Metrorail stations plus the Georgetown intersection of Wisconsin Ave & M St), collected Oct 2016–Nov 2017, about 9 visits per station. The unit of observation is the station-visit. The five-station subsample (Farragut North, Farragut West, Gallery Place-Chinatown, McPherson Square, Metro Center) has 102 station-visits. (ii) Panhandler-level receipts data: 80 panhandler observations (76 unique) at the 5 subsample stations, collected Feb–Apr 2019. Each station was visited 4 times, all on the same dates, simultaneously from 7:30–11:30 a.m. In total 67.6 hours were observed, about 51 minutes per panhandler on average. Dollar receipts are available for 67 panhandlers: 82% of those approached accepted the $5 offer to count their money in front of the researchers, and the $5 is not counted as a receipt.
- **Variable construction.** Dependent variable for Table 2: number of panhandlers counted within one square block of the station exit(s) on each visit (mean 1.066, SD 1.582). Key regressor: number of passersby, built from WMATA's monthly exit counts for the station in the month of the visit. Georgetown is given Foggy Bottom-GWU's ridership. The table reports it in units with mean 348.95 and SD 168.05, which looks like thousands of exits per month. Other controls:
  - Passerby friendliness: station average of each passerby's friendliest response on a 1–5 scale. It comes from 701 passersby approached for directions during 93 of the visits, over three train arrivals per visit.
  - Homeless service dummy: 1 if a shelter or soup kitchen is within a 10-minute walk.
  - Homeless shuttle stop dummy: 1 if a shuttle stop is within a 10-minute walk.

  Receipts outcomes: donations per hour (count of donations ÷ hours observed; mean 2.804, SD 3.175, N=80) and dollars per hour (mean 6.096, SD 11.039, N=67). In-kind donations are valued at market prices.
