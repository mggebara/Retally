Methodology Summary: # Methodology Summary

- **Data collection**: Observational field data on (a) panhandler counts at 26 locations (25 DC Metrorail stations + Georgetown intersection) via 242 station-visits over 10 months in 2016–2017, and (b) hourly panhandling receipts at a 5-station downtown subsample (Farragut North, Farragut West, Gallery Pl-Chinatown, McPherson Square, Metro Center) via 67.6 hours of covert observation of 80 panhandlers across 4 visits per station in Feb–Apr 2019. Receipts were verified by paying panhandlers $5 to count donations on-site; in-kind donations monetized at market prices.

- **Rationality regression (Table 2)**: OLS of *number of panhandlers at station-visit* on *number of monthly exiting passersby* (headline regressor, unstandardised level-level specification), *passerby friendliness* (1–5 scale from solicitation experiment with 701 passersby), and indicators for proximity (≤10-min walk) to a *homeless service* and *homeless shuttle stop*. Includes **date fixed effects**; **robust standard errors clustered by Metro station**. Estimated separately on full sample (N=242) and 5-station subsample (N=102). Standardised-beta effects reported in text as an auxiliary interpretation only.

- **Sample restriction for equalisation tests**: Restricted to 5 clustered downtown stations (each within 24-min walk of the others and 14-min walk of a homeless shuttle stop) to approximate low-cost arbitrage between stations; all 5 stations visited simultaneously 7:30–11:30 a.m. on identical dates.

- **Equality-of-returns tests (Tables 3–5)**: Three cross-station nonparametric/ANOVA tests on hourly receipts (donations/hour, N=80; dollars/hour, N=67):
  - **Brown-Forsythe** test for equality of variances (robust to skew; Levene's used as robustness check)
  - **One-way ANOVA** F-test for equality of means
  - **Kruskal-Wallis** chi-squared test for equality of medians

- **Robustness and power**: Results re-run bounding observed working minutes at lower bound of 10 minutes and upper bounds of 2 SD (144.6 min) and 3 SD (191.55 min) above the mean. Power calculations performed for the ANOVA under three variance scenarios (sample estimate; between-variance ±15%, within-variance ∓15%).

- **Evaluation protocol**: Hypothesis is failure to reject equality (p-values reported in parentheses in Tables 3–5); evidence of maximisation inferred from (i) significant positive passerby coefficient in Table 2 and (ii) non-rejection of equal variances/means/medians of hourly receipts across the 5 subsample stations.
