Methodology Summary: - **Data / samples.** (i) Station-visit panel: 242 visits to 26 "stations" (25 Washington DC Metrorail stations plus the Georgetown intersection of Wisconsin Ave & M St), collected Oct 2016–Nov 2017, about 9 visits per station. The unit of observation is the station-visit. The five-station subsample (Farragut North, Farragut West, Gallery Place-Chinatown, McPherson Square, Metro Center) has 102 station-visits. (ii) Panhandler-level receipts data: 80 panhandler observations (76 unique) at the 5 subsample stations, collected Feb–Apr 2019. Each station was visited 4 times, all on the same dates, simultaneously from 7:30–11:30 a.m. In total 67.6 hours were observed, about 51 minutes per panhandler on average. Dollar receipts are available for 67 panhandlers: 82% of those approached accepted the $5 offer to count their money in front of the researchers, and the $5 is not counted as a receipt.
- **Variable construction.** Dependent variable for Table 2: number of panhandlers counted within one square block of the station exit(s) on each visit (mean 1.066, SD 1.582). Key regressor: number of passersby, built from WMATA's monthly exit counts for the station in the month of the visit. Georgetown is given Foggy Bottom-GWU's ridership. The table reports it in units with mean 348.95 and SD 168.05, which looks like thousands of exits per month. Other controls:
  - Passerby friendliness: station average of each passerby's friendliest response on a 1–5 scale. It comes from 701 passersby approached for directions during 93 of the visits, over three train arrivals per visit.
  - Homeless service dummy: 1 if a shelter or soup kitchen is within a 10-minute walk.
  - Homeless shuttle stop dummy: 1 if a shuttle stop is within a 10-minute walk.

  Receipts outcomes: donations per hour (count of donations ÷ hours observed; mean 2.804, SD 3.175, N=80) and dollars per hour (mean 6.096, SD 11.039, N=67). In-kind donations are valued at market prices.
- **Estimating equation (Table 2).** OLS in levels, not logs and not standardised:
  NumPanhandlers_sv = β1·Passersby + β2·Friendliness + β3·ShuttleStop + β4·HomelessService + date FE + ε.
  - Col (1), full sample, N=242: passersby 0.005 (SE 0.001); friendliness 1.595 (0.851); shuttle 0.781 (0.182); service 0.450 (0.278); adjusted R² 0.39.
  - Col (2), five-station subsample, N=102: passersby 0.006 (0.002); friendliness 1.462 (2.793); shuttle 0.890 (0.227); service 0.815 (0.525); adjusted R² 0.06.

  The headline coefficients are unstandardised. The text also gives standardised effects: a 1 SD rise in passersby goes with a 0.53 SD (full sample) and 0.40 SD (subsample) rise in panhandlers.
- **Fixed effects and standard errors.** Both columns include date fixed effects (dates of visits). Standard errors are robust and clustered by Metro station: 26 clusters in col (1) and 5 in col (2). The paper says nothing about any extra sample restrictions.
- **Tests of rate-of-return equalisation (Tables 3–5).** All tests compare the 5 stations, run separately for donations/hour (Panel A, N=80) and dollars/hour (Panel B, N=67).
  - Table 3, equal variances: Brown-Forsythe test (Levene with median centring). W = 0.541 (p = 0.706) for donations and 0.276 (p = 0.893) for dollars. Footnote 16 says results are qualitatively the same with Levene's mean-based test.
  - Table 4, equal means: one-way ANOVA. F = 0.40 (p = 0.809) with df (4, 75) for donations; F = 0.15 (p = 0.962) with df (4, 62) for dollars. Sums of squares: 16.569 / 779.724 / 796.293 for donations and 77.539 / 7,965.676 / 8,043.215 for dollars. The paper's row labels "within" and "between" are swapped: the 4-df row is actually the between-station row.
  - Table 5, equal medians: Kruskal-Wallis χ² adjusted for ties. 2.506 (p = 0.644) for donations and 3.170 (p = 0.530) for dollars. Overall medians are 1.847 donations/hour and $1.395/hour.
  - Station means, SDs and Ns:

    - Dollars: Ns are 11 / 9 / 15 / 16 / 16 in the same order.
- **Robustness (footnote 17).** Tables 3–5 are re-run with the minutes observed bounded:
  - Bound set 1: a lower bound of 10 minutes, and an upper bound at mean + 3 SD (191.55 minutes).
  - Bound set 2: the same 10-minute lower bound, and an upper bound at mean + 2 SD (144.6 minutes).
  - Each bound is also applied on its own (only the lower or only the upper bound).

  Results are qualitatively unchanged.
- **Power analysis.** Power of the ANOVA at the 5% level, using the actual group sizes, under three assumptions about the ratio of between to within variance: equal to the sample estimate, 0.74× the sample estimate (within +15%, between −15%), and 1.35× the sample estimate.
  - Donations/hour: power 0.997, 0.978 and 1.
  - Dollars/hour: power 0.684, 0.535 and 0.826.
- **Evaluation and inference.** This is not a prediction exercise, so there is no train/test split, cross-validation or hyperparameters. Inference rests on coefficient signs and significance from the clustered SEs in Table 2, and on failing to reject equal variances, means and medians across stations in Tables 3–5 (all p > 0.5).
