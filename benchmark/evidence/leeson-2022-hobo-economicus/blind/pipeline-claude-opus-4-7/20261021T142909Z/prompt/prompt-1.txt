Methodology Summary: # Methodology Summary

- **Data collection**: Observational data on 242 Metro station-visits across 26 locations (25 Metrorail stations + Georgetown intersection) over 10 months in 2016–2017 to count panhandlers; a 5-station downtown DC subsample (Farragut North, Farragut West, Gallery Pl-Chinatown, McPherson Square, Metro Center) visited 4 times each over 3 months in 2019 (7:30–11:30 a.m., simultaneous visits) to record 67.6 hours of panhandling, yielding 80 panhandler observations. Panhandlers were counted within a one square-block area around station exits; receipts were verified by paying panhandlers $5 to count donations in front of the observer (82% acceptance). In-kind donations were monetized at market prices.

- **Variable construction**: Passerby friendliness measured via a standardized solicitation ("directions to a local landmark") across 3 train arrivals, coded on a 1–5 scale averaged by station. Panhandling opportunities proxied by WMATA monthly station exit counts. Indicator variables (= 1 if within a 10-minute walk) constructed via Google Maps for proximity to homeless services and to homeless shuttle stops.

- **Regression specification (Table 2)**: OLS of the number of panhandlers at each station-visit on the number of passersby (level, not logged), passerby friendliness, homeless shuttle stop indicator, and homeless service indicator. Date fixed effects included. Standard errors are robust and clustered by Metro station. Headline coefficients are unstandardized (the paper additionally describes a one-SD-in / one-SD-out interpretation). Estimated separately on the full sample (n=242) and the 5-station subsample (n=102).

- **Equality-of-returns tests (Tables 3–5)**: Hourly panhandling receipts (donations/hour and dollars/hour) compared across the 5 subsample stations using:
  - Brown-Forsythe test for equality of variances (robust to skew; Levene's test used as a check),
  - One-way ANOVA F-test for equality of means,
  - Kruskal-Wallis test for equality of medians.

- **Sample restrictions / rationale for subsample**: 5-station cluster chosen because stations are within 24-minute walk of each other and within 14-minute walk of a homeless shuttle stop, approximating low-cost arbitrage conditions needed for rate-of-return equalization.

- **Robustness checks**: Analyses in Tables 3–5 repeated bounding observed working minutes at 10 (lower) and at either 2 SD (144.6 min) or 3 SD (191.55 min) above the mean (upper). Power calculations for the ANOVA test performed under three assumptions about within/between variance ratios (sample estimate; ±15% perturbations yielding ratios of 0.74× and 1.35× the sample estimate).
