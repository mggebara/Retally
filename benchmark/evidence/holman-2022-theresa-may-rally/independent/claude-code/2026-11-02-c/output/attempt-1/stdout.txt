We recommend that files and replication code are downloaded as a single zip file from the dataverse. We recommend using Stata to replicate the files and establishing a working directory on your computer with the datasets to complete the analysis. 

Replication code, which will draw in the data files in the order necessary to produce the analysis in the paper. 

replication_files.do 

Data files. 

BES_data.dta 
did_data.dta
experimental2012.dta
constituency_results_2017_election.dta
global_approval.dta
BESExpertSurveyTrendFile2014-2019.dta
CMPdata.dta 
currency_rates.csv 

The CSES data on party likeability must be downloaded directly from their website. https://cses.org/data-download/cses-integrated-module-dataset-imd 

BES, did, constituency_results, and BESExpertSurvey data are all from the British Elections Study https://www.britishelectionstudy.com/data

experimental2012 data is generated by the authors. The study was approved by institutional review boards at Claremont Graduate University and Vanderbilt universities. It adhered to APSA’s Principle and Guidelines for Human Subjects Research. Participants were compensated for participation by the firm, as agreed upon when they opt-in to the firm’s panel.

global_approval data combines data from the Executive Approval Project http://www.executiveapproval.org/, the Global Terrorism Database https://www.start.umd.edu/gtd/, Penn World Tables  https://www.rug.nl/ggdc/productivity/pwt/?lang=en, the Database of Political Institutions https://www.iadb.org/en/research-and-data/dpi2020, and the World Bank www.worldbank.org 
currency_rates.csv are derived from OFX.com
