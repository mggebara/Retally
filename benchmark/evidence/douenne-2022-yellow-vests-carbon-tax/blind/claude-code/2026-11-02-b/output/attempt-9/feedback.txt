Crash (exit 1) in the Table 8 IV: "All observations contain NAs. Estimation cannot be done." preceded by "NAs introduced by coercion".
Cause: apres_modifs (the effectiveness-information indicator) is stored as "TRUE"/"FALSE" strings after stripping memisc attributes, so as.numeric() gave NA for every row.
Fix: convert binary indicators with a helper that maps "TRUE"/"1" to 1 and everything else to 0 (applied to all 0/1 and logical indicators). No specification change.
