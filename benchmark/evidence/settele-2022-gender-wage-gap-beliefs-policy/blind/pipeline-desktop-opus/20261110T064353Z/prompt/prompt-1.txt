Methodology Summary: - **Design / data:** Pre-registered online survey experiment (AEA RCT Registry AEARCTR-0003252) run with pureprofile in two waves (Wave A: 31 Aug–9 Oct 2018; Wave B: 21 Nov 2018–2 Jan 2019). Final sample N = 4,065 US respondents aged 18–65, quota-representative on gender, age, census region, employment status, political orientation and household income. An obfuscated follow-up survey about 2 weeks later went only to treated respondents (T74 and T94); follow-up sample N = 1,105. Respondents are randomized into T74 (ACS 2016 information: women earn $74 per $100 earned by men, among 45-year-old full-time employees with a Bachelor's degree), T94 (CPS Oct 2017 information: $94), or a pure control group with no information. Prior beliefs are incentivized at random for about half of respondents ($2 bonus if within $2 of the benchmark).
- **Outcomes:** Self-reported views on categorical scales: the wage gap is large, it is a problem, government should do more. Five-point support for specific policies: gender quotas, affirmative action, equal pay legislation, wage transparency (Wave A only), a reporting website (Wave B only), and child-care subsidies. Behavioral measures: signing a progressive or conservative White House petition, measured at group level through copied petition URLs, and a donation of $0–$300 from a $300 lottery prize to AAUW with a 50% match. Mechanism items: perceived drivers of the gap (Wave A), effectiveness of government (Wave B), and fairness. Posterior beliefs are elicited for one of five randomly assigned related wage statistics.
- **Preprocessing:** Qualitative outcomes are z-scored using the mean and SD of the pure control group. Follow-up outcomes are z-scored using the full follow-up sample. Summary indices are built for three outcome families: concern, specific policy demand, and perceived impersonal drivers (Anderson 2008). Partisanship is coded Democrat (incl. leaning Democrat), Republican (incl. leaning Republican), other Independent, and "other". For the correlational analysis only (a deviation from the pre-analysis plan), observations below the 5th or above the 95th percentile of prior beliefs are dropped; the 3rd/97th percentiles are used as a robustness check.
- **Main causal specification (Eq. 1):** OLS of Y_i = β0 + β1·T74_i + Θ'X_i + u_i, estimated on the two treatment arms only (pure control excluded). Y_i is in standardized units for the qualitative outcomes; T74 is a dummy, so β1 is the T74 vs. T94 difference. Controls X_i (pre-specified): survey wave, gender, prior belief, census region, five age categories, having at least one child, log household income, at least a 2-year college degree, employment-status dummies (full-time, part-time, self-employed, unemployed, student, out of labor force/retired), and political-orientation dummies (Democrat, Republican, Independent, other). No fixed effects beyond these dummies.
- **Inference / weights:** Heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors, no clustering. Probability weights in all regressions correct for accidental oversampling of young women.
- **Heterogeneity (Eq. 2):** Y_i = β0 + β1·H_i + β2·T74_i + β3·T74_i·H_i + Θ'X_i + u_i, with H_i for gender, partisanship, age, and recent news consumption (the last not pre-specified).
- **Scaling / IV:** 2SLS in which posterior beliefs about women's relative wages (standardized) are instrumented with T74 assignment, interpreted as a scaling exercise. A back-of-the-envelope calculation scales the treatment effect to the partisan and gender gaps in prior beliefs. Descriptive analysis: OLS of prior beliefs on gender, partisanship and their interaction, employment and education, and of control-group outcomes on beliefs, gender and party.
- **Multiple testing and robustness:** FWER-adjusted p-values for the three family indices and Anderson (2008) sharpened FDR q-values for individual outcomes. Robustness checks: persistence in the obfuscated follow-up, placebo outcomes, specifications including the pure control group (e.g., signal minus prior as the regressor), results by wave, and balance and attrition checks.
