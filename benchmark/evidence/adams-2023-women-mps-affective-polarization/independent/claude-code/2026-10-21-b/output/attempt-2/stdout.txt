===== Codebook for Dyadic Party Dataset.docx 
 Codebook for Dyadic Party Dataset (dyadic_data_1-4-22.Rdata)
Note – The structure of the dataset is directed dyads, meaning that each row captures the relationship between two parties, with a From party (the evaluating party) and a To party (the evaluated party).
Note – If a variable has _s after it in the dataset, it is the same variable but has been standardized to have a mean of 0 and a standard deviation of 1. 
country – Name of Country 
year – Year of Election 
cntryyr – Name of Country and Year of election 
to_mp_number – The manifestos project (https://manifesto-project.wzb.eu/) number for the To Party 
from_mp_number – The manifestos project (https://manifesto-project.wzb.eu/) number for the From Party 
party_from – The letter assigned to the “from” party in the CSES codebook – used for merging with CSES 
party_to – Same as above, for to party 
to_parfam – The party family of the “To” Party, following the the comparative manifestos project (https://manifesto-project.wzb.eu/) codebook 
10 = Green 
20 = Rad Left/Socialist30 = Social Democratic/Center-Left 40 = Liberal50 = Christian Democratic60 = Conservative70 = Radical Right80 = Agrarian90 = Regionalist95 = Misc/Other 
from_parfam – The party family of the from party, see above for codes 
party_like the mean thermometer score from partisans of the from party  assigned to the to party, drawn from the Comparative Study of Electoral Systems (10 = positive feelings and 0 = negative feelings). See https://cses.org for more
party_dislike – the mean thermometer score from partisans of the from party  assigned to the to party. Again fromt he CSES, rescaled so that 0 = positive feelings and 10 = negative feelings 
to_pfeml – The percentage of evaluated party’s parliamentary delegation who are women 1-year before the election, coded continuously from 0-1 (0= 0% Women MPs, 1=100% of delegation is women)
from_pfeml – The percentage of evaluating party’s parliamentary delegation who are women 1-year before the election, coded continuously from 0-1 (0= 0% Women MPs, 1=100% of delegation is women)
diff_pfeml – The absolute difference between the percentage of the evaluated party and the evaluating party’s parliamentary delegations who are women 1-year before the election, coded continuously from 0-1 (0= equal percentage of Women MPs, 1= one party has 0 women MPs, the other has 100% women MPs)
to_femaleleader– Is the leader of the party being evaluated female? (1= yes, 0=no)
rile (to/from/distance) – Rile is the unidimensional left right measure of party ideologiy from the Comparative Manifestos Project. For more details see (https://manifesto-project.wzb.eu/datasets)
prior_coalition – Were the parties in government together during the period before the election survey (1 = yes, 0 = no)
prior_opposition – Were the parties in opposition together during the period before the election survey (1 = yes, 0 = no)
to_left_bloc – Is the to party on the left of the country’s political system (1 = yes, 0 = no)
to_right_bloc – Is the to party on the right of the country’s political system (1 = yes, 0 = no) 
DM – The average district magnitude in the country during that election year
logDM -  the natural log of the average district magnitude
to_rrparty / from_rrparty – Is the party radical right (1 =yes, 0 = no) 
to_prior_seats – The number of parliamentary seats (lower chamber) held by the to-party prior to the election  
from_prior_seats – The number of parliamentary seats (lower chamber) held by the from-party prior to the election  
partydyad – ID variable for the specific dyad of from-to party in the dataset, used to cluster standard errors at the party-dyad level. Coded in terms of the CMP identifier of the from party and CMP identifier of the to-party.  

Variables not used in analysis but present in dataset:
from_senior_partner – Is the from party the senior partner in government (1 = yes, 0 = no) 
to_senior_partner – Is the to party the senior partner in government (1 = yes, 0 = no) 
To_in_gov – Is the to party in the governing coalition (1 = yes, 0 = no) 
From_in_gov – Is the from party in the governing coalition (1 = yes, 0 = no) 
Govern_together – Are the parties in government together (1 = yes, 0 = no) 
Opposition_together – Are the parties in opposition together (1 = yes, 0 = no) 
From_left_bloc – Is the from party on the left of the country’s political system (1 = yes, 0 = no) 
From_right_bloc – Is the from party on the right of the country’s political system (1 = yes, 0 = no) 
Same_bloc – Are the parties on the same side of the country’s political system (1 = yes, 0 = no) 
Nationalism (To/From/Distance) – A score derived from Burgoon (2009) using CMP to measure how nationalist a party is. Primary use would be for a robustness check of our “society” score. 
Rile (To/From/Distance) – Rile is the unidimensional left right measure of party ideologiy from the CMP 
ElecDate – the date of the election, useful if there are two elections in one country year 
Sat_together10 – Have the parties been in government together in the last 10 years 
Sat_together15 – Have the parties been in government together in the last 15 years 
Sat_together20 – Have the parties been in government together in the last 20 years 
Galtan(to/from/distance) – A measure of “cultural” ideology from the CHES. Has limited coverage (1999-2015, EU only) 
Lrecon(to/from/distance) - A measure of “economic” distance from the CHES. Has limited coverage (1999-2015, EU only) 
Multiculturalism - A measure of “multicultural” ideology from the CHES. Has limited coverage (2006-2015, EU only) 
Immigration_policy - A measure of “immigration” ideology from the CHES. Has limited coverage (2006-2015, EU only) 
Govern_together_10 – Have the parties been in government in the last 10 years, not including the current coalition (1 =yes, 0 = no) 
Govern_together_20 – Have the parties been in government in the last 20 years, but not in the most recent 10. (1 =yes, 0 = no) 
Economy(to/from/distance) – Our main measure of economic policy ideology, comes from the CMP. Details are here: https://manifesto-project.wzb.eu/information/documents/visualizations 
Society(to/from/distance) – Our main measure of “cultural” distance, comes from the CMP. Details are here: https://manifesto-project.wzb.eu/information/documents/visualizations
