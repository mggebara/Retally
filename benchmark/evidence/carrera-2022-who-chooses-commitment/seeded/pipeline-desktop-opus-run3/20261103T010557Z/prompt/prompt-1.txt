Methodology Summary: - **Design / sample:** Randomized field experiment with N = 1,248 members of a university-affiliated fitness facility in a Midwest U.S. city (aged 18+, members for the past 8 weeks). It ran in three waves between Oct 2015 and Mar 2016 (350, 528 and 414 enrolled). Participants completed an online elicitation, and then their gym attendance (card swipes) was observed for the 4 weeks starting the following Monday. Rewards were paid by mailed check after the 4 weeks.
- **Randomized information treatment (50% probability, assigned before any elicitation):** In wave 1 it was a graph of the participant's weekly visits over the past 20 weeks. In waves 2–3 it was enhanced: participants entered their own estimate of past weekly visits and were told that prior participants overestimated by about 4 visits over 4 weeks. The treatment is used to identify the causal effect of sophistication on beliefs, on the behavior change premium and on commitment take-up.
- **Elicitations:**
  - Forecast visits (0–28) and a goal for the next 4 weeks.
  - For six piece-rate incentives ($1, $2, $3, $5, $7 and $12 per visit, shown in random order): forecasted attendance and willingness to pay (WTP), elicited with an incentive-compatible BDM slider ($0 up to 30× the rate, with a fill-in box at the maximum).
  - Binary choices between $80 unconditional and $80 conditional on attending ≥8, ≥12 or ≥16 times ("more" contracts), or ≤7, ≤11 or ≤15 times ("less" contracts), in waves 1–2. Wave 3 offered only ≥12 and ≤11, plus the participant's believed probability of meeting each threshold.
- **Random assignment of actual incentives:** One question was drawn per person, and the BDM fixed payment came from a mixture: U[$0,$7] with weight 0.99 and U[full slider range] with weight 0.01.
  - Wave 1: $0, $2 and $7 per visit, each with probability 0.33.
  - Wave 2: $0 (33%), $2 (33%), $5 (16.5%) and $7 (16.5%).
  - Wave 3: $0, $7 per visit, and $80 for 12+ visits, each 33%.
  - In every wave the remaining 1% was spread over all other questions.
- **Sample restriction:** 44 observations where assignment could be endogenous are excluded throughout. These are 12 whose fixed-payment draw was above $7 and 32 whose WTP fell inside the $0–$7 range.
- **Reduced-form estimation:**
  - Behavior change premium: BCP(p,Δ) = [w(p+Δ) − w(p)]/Δ − [α̃(p+Δ) + α̃(p)]/2, built from adjacent incentive levels and expressed per $1 of incentive. Its headline mean is $1.78 per visit.
  - Effects of the randomized incentives on actual visits are estimated by comparing assigned groups. Headline: +3.51 visits for the $80/12+ contract.
  - Take-up rates of the "more" contracts (64%, 49% and 32%) and the "less" contracts (27–34%).
  - Correlation between "more" and "less" take-up, and the association of take-up with the BCP and other sophistication proxies.
  - Effect of the information treatment on forecasts, BCP and take-up.
- **Structural estimation (Section 7):** A quasi-hyperbolic (β–δ, with δ = 1) model with partial naivete (β̃), following O'Donoghue–Rabin (2001), with stochastic costs and quasilinear utility. It is fit to forecasted attendance, WTP and actual attendance at the randomized piece rates. Contract take-up is modelled with mean-zero stochastic valuation errors (logit, "imperfect perception").
  - Estimates: β = 0.55, β̃ = 0.84 (0.86 in control vs 0.78 under information), b = $9.66 per visit, internality (1−β)b = $4.39.
  - The model is validated with out-of-sample tests.
  - It is used to compute consumer surplus from the contracts under the long-run criterion: −$7.91, −$18.69 and −$10.51 per taker for 8+, 12+ and 16+. It also compares the social efficiency of the contracts with linear per-visit subsidies scaled to produce the same average attendance change.
- **Not available in the supplied text:** The paper text is cut off at the start of Section 4 (Data). The exact regression specifications, control sets, fixed effects and standard-error clustering therefore cannot be reported. There is no ML train/test split or cross-validation.

*** (CCtable.tex) *************************************************************
*** This table was created by hand, so no tex output is produced for it *******

local commitlist "8 12 16"

*** Summarize take-up of commitment contracts ***
foreach X of local commitlist {
  * Declare local for labeling
  if `X' == 8 loc num "eight"
  else if `X' == 12 loc num "twelve"
  else loc num "sixteen"
  * Chose "more" contract
  summarize commit`X'
  * Store mean and number of observations
  loc takeup = round(r(mean)*100)
  loc takeup : di %2.0f `takeup'
  latex_write commit`num'rate `takeup' numbers
  local obsnum : di %5.0fc `r(N)'
  if r(N) > 1000 loc obsnum = subinstr("`obsnum'",",","{,}",.)
  latex_write commit`num'obs `obsnum' numbers
  * Chose "fewer" contract
  summarize anticommit`X'
  * Chose "more" given chose "fewer"
  summarize commit`X' if anticommit`X'==1
  * Chose "fewer" given chose "more"
  summarize anticommit`X' if commit`X'==1
  * Difference between "more" and "more" given chose "fewer" means
  gen commit`X'_cdtn = commit`X' if anticommit`X'==1
  ttest commit`X'_cdtn = commit`X', unpaired
  * Difference between "fewer" and "fewer" given chose "more" means
  gen anticommit`X'_cdtn = anticommit`X' if commit`X'==1
  ttest anticommit`X'_cdtn = anticommit`X', unpaired
}

*** (regs_{avg_delta_ex_1,commit}_all.tex) ************************************
	
*** Generate variable for expected attendace w/incentive given
gen days_exp = .
foreach i in 0 1 2 3 5 7{
	replace days_exp = days_`i' if incentive == `i'
	}
	
*** Generate variables for gaps between goal, past, expected, & actual attendance
gen gap_goal_exp = goal - days_0 // under no incentive
gen gap_goal_past = goal - past4
gen gap_actual_exp = visits - days_exp


*** Generate behavior change premium variables (second-order approximation)
gen delta1=(wtp1) - (days_1+days_0)/2
gen delta2 = (wtp2-wtp1) - (days_2+days_1)/2
gen delta3=(wtp3-wtp2) - (days_3+days_2)/2
gen delta5=(wtp5-wtp3)/(5-3) - (days_5+days_3)/2
gen delta7=(wtp7-wtp5)/(7-5) - (days_7+days_5)/2
gen delta12=(wtp12-wtp7)/(12-7) - (days_12+days_7)/2
gen avg_delta_ex1 = (delta2 + delta3 + delta5 + delta7 + delta12)/5
	

** Store number of observations without an incentive
gen got_contract = (incentive == .)
sum got_contract
latex_write gotcontractobs `r(sum)' numbers


*** Perform series of analyses for whole sample

* Declare restriction for following analysis
loc restrict "!missing(incentive)"
	
* Store means and standard deviations
foreach v in gap_goal_exp gap_actual_exp{
	if "`v'" == "gap_goal_exp" loc name "goalexp"
	else loc name "actexp"
	sum `v' if `restrict'
	loc mean : di %4.2f r(mean)
	loc sd : di %4.2f r(sd)
	latex_write `name'mean `mean' numbers
	latex_write `name'sd `sd' numbers
	}
	
* Generate z-scores for variables with constant restriction
foreach dvar in gap_goal_exp gap_actual_exp avg_delta_ex1{
	sum `dvar' if `restrict'
	gen z_`dvar' = (`dvar' - r(mean)) / r(sd) if `restrict'
	}
	
* Run regressions of behavior change premium with regressors & constant restriction
eststo clear
loc k = 1
foreach controls in "" "female i.income_cat married ft_student fpt_working advanced i.age_cat"{
	foreach covar in "first_info new_info" "first_info new_info z_gap_goal_exp" "first_info new_info z_gap_actual_exp"{

		* Run regression
		reg avg_delta_ex1 wave2 wave3 `covar' `controls' if `restrict', r
		eststo spec_`k'
		
		* Store coefficients from certain regressions
		if `k' == 1{
			loc coeff : di %4.2f _b[new_info]
			latex_write enhinfobcpcoeff `coeff' numbers
			}
		else if `k' == 2{
			loc coeff : di %4.2f _b[z_gap_goal_exp]
			latex_write goalexpbcpcoeff `coeff' numbers
			}
		else if `k' == 3{
			loc coeff : di %4.2f _b[z_gap_actual_exp]
			latex_write actualexpbcpcoeff `coeff' numbers
			}
		
		* Store dependent variable mean
		ci means avg_delta_ex1 if `restrict' & !missing(gap_actual_exp)
		local m : di %4.2f r(mean)
		local se : di %4.2f r(se)
		estadd local dvmean `m'
		estadd local dvse "(`se')"
		ci means avg_delta_ex1 if `restrict' & !missing(gap_actual_exp) & ///
			control_info == 1
		local mcontrol : di %4.2f r(mean)
		local secontrol : di %4.2f r(se)
		estadd local dvmeancontrol `mcontrol'
		if `k' == 1 latex_write bcpinfoctrlmean `mcontrol' numbers
		estadd local dvsecontrol "(`secontrol')"
		estadd local wavefe "Yes"
		if `k' == 1 latex_write bcpregmean `m' numbers
		if "`controls'" != "" estadd local ctrl "Yes"
		
		loc ++k
		}
	}
