## Paper
Title: Cooperative Property Rights
Methodology: - **Design / estimator:** Sharp (reduced-form) regression discontinuity at the 500 ha cumulative-landholding threshold of Decree 153 (El Salvador 1980 land reform, phase I). Estimating equation (1): y_po = α + γ·Above500_o + f(holdings_o) + ε_po for o ∈ RS, where y_po is the outcome for plot/property p owned before the reform by owner o, Above500_o = 1(cumulative landholdings_o ≥ 500 ha) (owner's total holdings, not the plot's own size), and f(·) is the RD polynomial in the former owner's total landholdings (running variable, centred at 500 ha). Coefficient of interest γ = effect of being subject to expropriation/reorganisation as a cooperative vs. remaining an outside-owned hacienda. Headline coefficients are in the outcome's natural units (only the geographic balance figure, Fig. 4, reports standardised β coefficients).
- **Polynomial, bandwidth, kernel:** Baseline follows Calonico, Cattaneo & Titiunik (2014a,b) / Calonico et al. (2017): local linear polynomial estimated separately on each side of the cutoff, with the MSE-optimal bandwidth (rdrobust-style) defining the risk set RS of former owners near 500 ha. Appendix J checks robustness to other polynomial orders and a range of bandwidths. The first-stage figure (Fig. 5) instead uses a local quadratic on each side within a fixed 300 ha bandwidth, uniform kernel, 25 ha bins.
- **Fuzzy RD / IV:** Compliance was imperfect (≈75 percentage-point jump in expropriation probability at the threshold; ~20% of above-threshold properties stayed haciendas, a few below-threshold properties were expropriated), so the paper also reports fuzzy RD (scaled IV) estimates, instrumenting "reform cooperative" status with Above500_o.
- **Standard errors:** Clustered at the former-landholder (owner) level, since one owner can hold several plots and treatment depends on the owner's total holdings.
- **Data and sample construction:** Prereform (1980) landholdings from ISTA records (owners of expropriated properties) and Figueroa Aquino & Marroquín Mena (1991) (all non-expropriated owners with >100 ha cumulative holdings); expropriation lists from MAG (1983). These are matched by property name, municipality, department and size in hectares to producers in the 2007–08 IV Census of Agriculture (94,168 producers); about 70% are matched, with no discontinuity in the match probability at the threshold (Fig. D3). The census cooperative indicator separates cooperatives from haciendas. Estimation sample = matched properties of former owners whose cumulative holdings lie within the bandwidth around 500 ha. The worker analysis uses EHPM household surveys 2002–2013, matched to properties only in cantons with a single cooperative/large hacienda (91% accuracy when checked against the 2008–2010 surveys that report property names).
- **Outcomes (Table 1 tests):** (1) share of land devoted to cash crops (sugarcane, coffee, etc.) vs. staple crops (maize, beans, etc.); (2) cash crop yields; (3) staple crop yields; (4) inequality of worker earnings measured by the interquartile range of income for cooperative vs. hacienda workers. The paper also looks at aggregate productivity (revenue per ha, profit per ha), which is imprecisely estimated and inconclusive.
- **Identification checks:** A McCrary (2008) density test (the data are collapsed into landholding bins and the bin count is used as the outcome in eq. 1) finds no sorting (Fig. 3). Covariate balance comes from running eq. (1) on geographic characteristics: land suitability, precipitation, elevation, suitability for sugarcane/coffee/cotton and for maize/beans/rice/sorghum. Fig. 4 uses local linear and the MSE-optimal bandwidth and finds no discontinuities, though the estimates are imprecise. Further robustness checks (matching estimators, sensitivity to balance-CI bounds) are in Apps. D.2–D.3. The design has no train/test split or cross-validation. Inference is RD point estimates with clustered SEs (the CCT approach).

  d <- base %>% filter(abs(run) <= bw)
  if (any(is.na(d$survey_year))) {
    m <- feols(y ~ Above500 + run + run:Above500, data = d, cluster = ~cl)
  } else {
    m <- feols(y ~ Above500 + run + run:Above500 | survey_year, data = d, cluster = ~cl)

  print(summary(m))
  list(b = unname(coef(m)["Above500"]), n = nobs(m), ncl = length(unique(d$cl[obs(m)])))
}
