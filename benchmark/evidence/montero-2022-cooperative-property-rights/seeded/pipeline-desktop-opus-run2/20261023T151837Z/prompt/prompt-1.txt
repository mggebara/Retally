Title: Cooperative Property Rights
Abstract (first 500 chars): from one proposed ex-

Methodology Summary: - **Design / estimator:** Sharp-assignment regression discontinuity (RD) at the 500 ha cumulative-landholding threshold of the 1980 El Salvador land reform (Decree 153, Phase I). Equation (1): y_po = α + γ·Above500_o + f(holdings_o) + ε_po for owners o in the risk set RS, where y_po is the outcome for plot/property p owned by pre-reform owner o, Above500_o = 1(cumulative landholdings_o ≥ 500 ha) (owner-level, not property size), and f(·) is the RD polynomial in the former owner's total 1980 landholdings (running variable, centred at 500 ha). γ is the reduced-form (intent-to-treat) effect of being above the threshold on outcomes (cooperatives vs. outside-owned haciendas). Outcomes are in their natural units (land shares, yields, IQR of earnings); no standardisation of headline estimates is stated (only the geographic balance figure, Fig. 4, plots standardised coefficients).
- **Polynomial, kernel, bandwidth (baseline):** Following Calonico, Cattaneo & Titiunik (2014a,b) and Calonico et al. (2017), a local linear polynomial estimated separately on each side of the cutoff, with the MSE-optimal bandwidth (rdrobust-style); the risk set RS is owners within that bandwidth of 500 ha. Appendix J checks robustness to alternative polynomials and bandwidths. The first-stage RD plot (Fig. 5) instead uses a local quadratic on each side, a fixed 300 ha bandwidth, a uniform kernel, and 25 ha bins.
- **Fuzzy RD / IV:** Compliance is imperfect (above-threshold owners ~75 percentage points more likely to be expropriated; ~20% of above-threshold properties stayed haciendas, a few below-threshold ones were expropriated), so the paper also reports fuzzy-RD (scaled IV) estimates that instrument "reform cooperative_po" with Above500_o.
- **Standard errors:** Clustered at the former-landholder (pre-reform owner) level, since one owner may have held several plots and treatment is defined by the owner's total holdings.
- **Data and sample construction:** Pre-reform 1980 landholdings come from the Property Registry via ISTA records (expropriated owners) and Figueroa Aquino & Marroquín Mena (1991) (non-expropriated owners with >100 ha); the list of expropriated properties and cooperatives comes from MAG (1983). These are matched to the 2007–08 IV Agricultural Census (94,168 producers) by property name, municipality, department and size (~70% match rate, with no discontinuity in the match probability at the threshold, Fig. D3). The estimation sample is matched properties of owners with >100 ha whose holdings fall within the bandwidth around 500 ha. Worker outcomes use the 2002–2013 EHPM household surveys, with individuals matched to cooperatives or haciendas by canton, restricted to cantons with only one cooperative or large hacienda (91% matching accuracy checked against the 2008–2010 surveys).
- **Outcomes / evaluation (Table 1 predictions):** (1) share of land devoted to cash crops (sugarcane, coffee) vs. staple crops (maize, beans); (2) cash-crop yields; (3) staple-crop yields; (4) the interquartile range or distribution of worker earnings (more compressed in cooperatives). Aggregate productivity (revenue per ha, profits per ha) is also examined. Alternative mechanisms (credit access, migration) are tested in Section VII.
- **Validity checks:** A McCrary (2008) density test, run by collapsing the data into landholding bins and regressing bin counts on eq. (1) (Fig. 3), finds no sorting. Balance tests (Fig. 4) estimate eq. (1) on geographic covariates with a local linear specification and MSE-optimal bandwidth: land suitability, precipitation, elevation, suitability for sugarcane, coffee and cotton, and suitability for maize, beans, rice and sorghum. These show no significant discontinuities. Robustness checks include matching methods (App. D.2), bounds based on the edges of the balance-test confidence intervals (App. D.3), and alternative bandwidths and polynomials (App. J).
- **Controls / fixed effects:** The baseline specification includes only the RD polynomial in the running variable, with slopes estimated separately on each side. The supplied text names no additional fixed effects or covariates for the preferred specification. The text provided is truncated before the results tables, so the column-level control sets and point estimates are not available here.
