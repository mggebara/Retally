Methodology Summary: ## Methodology Summary

- **Design**: Sharp/fuzzy regression discontinuity (RD) exploiting the 500 ha cumulative-landholding threshold from El Salvador's 1980 Decree 153, which triggered expropriation and reorganization into cooperatives for owners above the cutoff.

- **Estimating equation**: `y_po = α + γ·Above500_o + f(holdings_o) + ε_po` for owners o in the RD risk set. Dependent variables include plot-level crop land shares, crop-specific yields, revenues/ha, and (in household data) worker income dispersion. Key regressor is an indicator for cumulative pre-reform landholdings ≥500 ha; running variable is the former owner's total 1980 landholdings. Fuzzy RD uses Above500 as an instrument for being a reform cooperative.

- **RD specification**: Local linear polynomials estimated separately on each side of the 500 ha cutoff, using the MSE-optimal bandwidth of Calonico, Cattaneo, and Titiunik (2014b) and Calonico et al. (2017). Robustness in Appendix J uses alternative polynomial orders and bandwidths; balance figure uses a uniform kernel; the first-stage figure uses a local quadratic within a fixed 300 ha bandwidth.

- **Standard errors**: Clustered at the former-landholder level (since owners could hold multiple plots and treatment is defined at the owner level).

- **Sample construction**: Pre-reform landholdings compiled from ISTA records (expropriated owners) and Figueroa Aquino & Marroquín Mena (1991) (non-expropriated owners with >100 ha); matched by name/municipality/department/size to the 2007–2008 IV Census of Agriculture (~70% match rate, with no discontinuity in match probability at the cutoff). Worker-level analysis uses Encuestas de Hogares de Propósitos Múltiples (2002–2013), restricted to cantons containing only one cooperative/large hacienda to enable matching.

- **Identification checks**: (i) McCrary (2008) density test for sorting at 500 ha; (ii) RD balance tests on geographic covariates (land suitability, precipitation, elevation, suitability for sugarcane, coffee, cotton, maize, beans, rice, sorghum), reported as standardized (β) RD coefficients; (iii) first-stage RD on an expropriation indicator; (iv) robustness via matching methods and sensitivity analysis bounding reform properties at the edges of balance-test CIs (Appendices D.2, D.3); (v) data-manipulation checks on reported crop output across ownership forms (Appendix C).

- **Outcomes tested** (mapped to model predictions in Table 1): share of land in cash vs. staple crops; yields for cash crops (sugarcane, coffee); yields for staple crops (maize, beans); aggregate revenues/ha and profits/ha; and interquartile range of worker earnings from household surveys.
