Methodology Summary: - **Identification and estimator:** The paper uses a regression discontinuity design at the 500 ha cumulative-landholding threshold from Decree 153 (1980 land reform, phase I).
  - The running variable is the former owner's total 1980 landholdings, not the size of the individual plot.
  - The estimating equation (eq. 1) is: y_po = α + γ·Above500_o + f(holdings_o) + ε_po, for owners o in the risk set RS.
  - Above500_o = 1(cumulative landholdings_o ≥ 500 ha).
  - There are no fixed effects or additional controls in the baseline. The only other term is the RD polynomial.
  - The baseline uses a local linear polynomial fitted separately on each side of the cutoff (Calonico, Cattaneo & Titiunik 2014).
  - The bandwidth is the MSE-optimal one from Calonico, Cattaneo & Titiunik (2014b) and Calonico et al. (2017).
- **Fuzzy RD:** Because compliance with the threshold was imperfect, the paper also reports scaled IV (fuzzy RD) estimates. Above500_o instruments for reform-cooperative status.
- **Standard errors:** Clustered at the former-landholder level, because one owner may have held multiple plots.
- **Data construction and sample:**
  - The list of expropriated properties comes from the Ministry of Agriculture's 1983 report (MAG 1983) and records from the El Salvador Institute for Agrarian Transformation (ISTA).
  - Landholdings of non-expropriated owners with more than 100 ha come from Figueroa Aquino & Marroquín Mena (1991).
  - Pre-reform properties are matched to the 2007–08 IV Census of Agriculture (94,168 producers) using property name, municipality, department and hectare size.
  - The paper checks that the probability of finding a match is continuous at the threshold and unrelated to geography.
- **Outcomes, mapped to model predictions (Table 1):**
  - Share of land devoted to cash crops (e.g., sugarcane, coffee) versus staple crops (e.g., maize, beans).
  - Crop-specific yields for cash and staple crops.
  - Aggregate revenue and profit per hectare.
  - Interquartile range of worker earnings.
- **Worker-earnings sample:** Earnings come from the EHPM household surveys, 2002–2013.
  - Workers are matched to cooperatives or haciendas using self-reported employment type and location.
  - Matching is restricted to cantons containing only one cooperative or large hacienda.
  - Match accuracy is validated with the 2008–2010 surveys, which include property names.
- **Validity checks:**
  - **Sorting:** A McCrary density test, implemented by using binned observation counts as the dependent variable in eq. (1).
  - **Geographic balance:** Eq. (1) is estimated with local linear polynomials and the MSE-optimal bandwidth on land suitability, precipitation, elevation, and suitability for sugarcane, coffee, cotton, maize, beans, rice and sorghum. These balance coefficients are reported as standardized (β); the headline outcome estimates are not described as standardized.
  - **First stage:** An expropriation indicator is regressed on a local quadratic polynomial, separately on each side of the cutoff, within a fixed 300 ha bandwidth. This uses a uniform kernel, 25 ha bins for plotting, and standard errors clustered by former owner.
- **Robustness:**
  - Alternative RD polynomials and bandwidths (App. J).
  - Matching estimators (App. D.2).
  - Sensitivity analysis assuming imbalance at the edges of the balance-test confidence intervals (App. D.3).
  - Checks for data manipulation in census output reports (App. C).
  - There is no train/test split or cross-validation; this is causal inference rather than predictive evaluation.
