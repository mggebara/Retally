Methodology Summary: - **Design / estimator:** Sharp (reduced-form) regression discontinuity design at the 500 ha cumulative-landholding threshold from Decree 153 (1980 El Salvador land reform, phase I). Estimating equation (1): y_po = α + γ·Above500_o + f(holdings_o) + ε_po for o ∈ RS, where y_po is the outcome for plot p owned by pre-reform owner o, Above500_o = 1(cumulative landholdings_o ≥ 500 ha) (owner's total holdings, not plot size), and f(·) is the RD polynomial in the former owner's total landholdings (running variable, centred at 500 ha). Coefficient of interest is γ (effect of eligibility for expropriation/cooperative formation relative to remaining an outside-owned hacienda); headline estimates are in the outcome's natural units (only the geographic balance figure, Fig. 4, plots standardized coefficients).
- **Baseline specification:** Local linear polynomial estimated separately on each side of the cutoff (Calonico, Cattaneo & Titiunik 2014a,b), with bandwidth chosen as the MSE-optimal bandwidth (CCT 2014b; Calonico et al. 2017), i.e. rdrobust-style estimation. Fuzzy RD / IV estimates are also reported, instrumenting "reform cooperative" status with Above500_o because compliance was imperfect. No additional controls or fixed effects in the baseline equation beyond the RD polynomial.
- **Standard errors:** Clustered at the former landholder (owner) level, since an owner may have held several plots and treatment is assigned at the owner level.
- **Data and sample construction:** Pre-reform (1980) landholdings from the Property Registry, combining ISTA records for owners of expropriated properties and Figueroa Aquino & Marroquín Mena (1991) for all non-expropriated owners with >100 ha cumulative holdings; expropriation list from MAG (1983) and ISTA former-owner records. Properties are matched to the 2007–2008 IV Census of Agriculture (94,168 producers) by property name, municipality, department and size (~70% matched; match probability shown to be continuous at the threshold). Estimation sample (risk set RS) = plots of former owners whose cumulative holdings lie within the bandwidth around 500 ha.
- **Outcomes (Table 1 predictions):** Share of land devoted to cash crops (sugarcane, coffee, cotton) vs. staple crops (maize, beans, rice, sorghum); crop-specific yields for cash and staple crops; aggregate productivity (revenues per ha, profits per ha); worker earnings equality (interquartile range of worker earnings) using the 2002–2013 Encuesta de Hogares de Propósitos Múltiples, with workers matched to cooperatives/haciendas only in cantons with a single cooperative/large hacienda (match accuracy checked against 2008–2010 surveys with property names).
- **Validity checks:** McCrary (2008) density test, collapsing data into landholding bins and using bin counts as the dependent variable in eq. (1); covariate balance by estimating eq. (1) on geographic characteristics (land suitability, precipitation, elevation, suitability for sugarcane, coffee, cotton, maize, beans, rice, sorghum) with local linear polynomials and MSE-optimal bandwidth; continuity of the probability of matching to the census.
- **First stage:** Expropriation indicator regressed on a local quadratic polynomial in former owner's total landholdings, separately on each side of 500 ha, within a fixed 300 ha bandwidth, uniform kernel, 25 ha bins for plotting, SEs clustered by former owner (Fig. 5).
- **Robustness:** Appendix J varies the RD polynomial order and bandwidths; Appendix D reports matching-method estimates and sensitivity bounds assuming covariates at the edge of balance-test confidence intervals. No train/test split or cross-validation (not applicable).
