Methodology Summary: - **Design / estimator:** Cross-sectional municipality-level IV (2SLS) design. The first stage (eq. 1, Table I) is an OLS regression of *Socialist vote share in 1919* on *Share of foot soldier casualties* (count of WWI foot soldier deaths from the municipality, taken from the military Roll of Honor and digitized by ISTORECO, divided by the male population over age six in the 1911 census) plus controls X_i. In the second stage, measures of fascist activity are regressed on the 1919 socialist vote share, instrumented by foot soldier casualties. The four outcomes are *Fascist Party branches* in September 1921, *Fascist violence in 1920–22* (episodes per 1,000 inhabitants, from Franzinelli 2003), and the *Fascist vote share* in 1921 and in 1924. Functional form is linear in levels and shares, with no logs on the dependent or key variables.
- **Units:** The paper explicitly states that all regressions report standardized coefficients, computed after standardizing both left-hand-side and right-hand-side variables. So the headline estimates are in standard-deviation units.
- **Fixed effects and controls:** Every specification includes province fixed effects, regiment fixed effects (dummies for any deaths from the municipality in a given regiment) and basic demographic controls (a quartic in log municipality population and the share of population under age six in 1911). Table I adds controls cumulatively:
  - (2) geographic controls: log area, elevation of the main center, maximum elevation.
  - (3) socialist vote share in 1913.
  - (4) military controls: veterans from the 1874–95 classes and from the 1896–1900 classes; special-assault-troop and volunteer casualties as a share of males over six; a dummy for army-supplying production plants; a dummy for any casualties in the highest-mortality battles (days with more than 1,000 casualties).
  - (5) agricultural controls: share of day laborers, share of sharecroppers, a dummy for a local landowner/agrarian association.
  - (6) urban controls: industrial workers and industrial firms over the 1911 male population, the 1911 literacy rate, the share of entrepreneurs and rentiers, the share of the bourgeoisie.

  Column (6) is the full control set.
- **Standard errors:** Heteroskedasticity-robust and clustered at the administrative-district level (181 districts). Conley spatial standard errors are reported as a robustness check in Online Appendix Table A2. The first-stage F-statistic is reported.
- **Sample:** 5,775 municipalities in 64 provinces (out of 8,355 municipalities in the 1921 census, excluding Julian Venetia and Trentino). These are the municipalities for which the authors recovered 1919–24 election results from newspapers and archives; parts of Calabria and Sicily are missing. All data are mapped to 1921 municipal boundaries. The 1921 fascist vote share has two versions. The restricted no-imputation sample of 2,188 municipalities uses municipality-level National Bloc candidate votes, with bloc votes allocated to fascists in proportion to the fascist candidates' share of candidate votes. The full version imputes the remaining municipalities from district, province or electoral-district data.
- **Variable construction and preprocessing:** Only foot soldier casualties are used; navy, air force, special assault troops and volunteers are excluded because of selection. Veteran shares come from draftees minus casualties by cohort at the military-district level and are assigned to all municipalities in that district. Census class shares (day laborers, sharecroppers, elites, bourgeoisie) are measured for roughly 700 agrarian zones and assigned to every municipality in the zone. Jewish deportation share is capped at 1. Alternative instruments:
  - Relative rainfall for Dec 1918–May 1919: station rainfall divided by the 1915–79 winter–spring average, interpolated to municipalities by inverse distance with a 30 km cutoff, and capped at 1.
  - Spanish-flu excess mortality in 1918 relative to 1911–14, available only for 207 urban municipalities.
- **Validation / evaluation protocol:**
  - Balance tests of the instrument against pre-1919 political, economic and social covariates.
  - Robustness to adding the control blocks one at a time.
  - Placebo and mechanism checks: 1919 Nationalist vote, WWI monuments by 1921, and volunteer and assault-troop casualties.
  - Alternative violence measures: fascist killings, all political violence, non-fascist violence.
  - Heterogeneity by prior socialist strength, landowner associations and elite share.
  - Alternative instruments (drought, Spanish flu).
  - Longer-run outcomes: Jewish deportations in 1943–45 and postwar (1946–2018) vote shares.
