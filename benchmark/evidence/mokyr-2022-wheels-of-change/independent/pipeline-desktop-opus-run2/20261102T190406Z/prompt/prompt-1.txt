Methodology Summary: - **Data / unit of analysis**: Cross-section of England's government-area districts (326 districts; 325 after dropping the Isles of Scilly for missing HYDE population data). Domesday Book (1086) coverage limits all regressions using DB mills to **298 districts** (London, Winchester, Bristol, Tamworth, Durham, Northumberland missing; Cumberland/Westmorland/Lancashire partial).
- **Variable construction (preprocessing)**: Dependent variable = number of apprentices to "wrights" (millwrights, wheelwrights, "wrights") from the Apprenticeship Stamp Tax registers (IR 1), 1710–50, master locations geocoded to TownsList places (10,201 of 36,144 locations; zero elsewhere), aggregated to district and divided by mean district population 1710–50 (HYDE) — **per 10,000 people**. Key regressor = number of Domesday Book (water)mills per capita, normalised by the same 1710–50 mean population (per 10,000). Textile proxy: apprentices to drapers/clothiers (4,359 people) per capita; weavers as alternative. Variables are in levels (linear–linear), not logs, and headline coefficients are unstandardised.
- **Baseline specification (Eq. 1, Table 2)**: OLS, w_i = β·Mills_i + X'γ + ε_i, with constant. Controls added stepwise: col (1) none; col (2) latitude, longitude (district centroid), mean ruggedness (TRI), mean elevation; col (3) + agricultural suitability, wheat suitability (FAO GAEZ), pasture suitability; col (4) + mean precipitation and temperature; col (5, preferred) + mean population 1710–50, minimal distance to an important harbour, distance to London, minimal distance to a Roman road, minimal distance to a navigable river, and district area. No fixed effects.
- **Headline estimates**: β = 0.15 (SE 0.02) unconditional; 0.14 (0.02) in cols 2–4; **0.12 (0.03)** in col 5, all significant at 1%; adj. R² 0.57–0.61; N = 298. Interpretation: one additional DB mill per 10,000 people → ~0.12–0.14 more wright apprentices per 10,000 in 1710–50.
- **Standard errors**: Heteroskedasticity-robust, **clustered at the (historical) county level** in all regressions.
- **IV strategy (Table 3)**: 2SLS instrumenting DB mills per capita with "grinding-mill suitability": length of rivers in the district with moderate ruggedness (TRI between 2 and 6) interacted with a high-wheat-suitability indicator (mean GAEZ wheat suitability category ≤ 5). River suitability and wheat suitability enter as separate controls. IV estimates: 0.16 (0.04) no controls (first-stage F = 19.90); 0.15 (0.04) with main geographic + agricultural + climatic controls (F = 16.96); 0.16 (0.04) adding other geographic controls (F = 16.17); corresponding OLS 0.15, 0.14, 0.12; N = 298.
- **Robustness / extensions**: Black Death test (Eq. 2): adds district population change 1300–1400 (HYDE) and its interaction with DB mills (coefficient of interest δ3); Winchester-district case study comparing rivers Meon vs Itchen (1086 vs 1759 mills); placebo regressions for industries without water-powered machinery; worsted-producer relocation; horserace of mills vs wrights; mediation analysis (wrights account for 30–70% of mills' total effect on industrialisation); bias-from-unobservables test (Section 5.4).
- **Evaluation**: No train/test split or cross-validation; inference via coefficient magnitude, clustered-SE significance (two-sided tests at 1/5/10%), adjusted R², and first-stage F-statistics for IV strength.
